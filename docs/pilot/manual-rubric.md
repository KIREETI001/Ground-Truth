# Manual diagnostic rubric

A human tutor reads authorized original work and actual receipts. Score each dimension **separately**, 0–3, for the main explanation and the independent transfer explanation. Plain language earns the same credit as technical terms. Missing evidence is `N/A`; record help and contamination separately. A correct count, term, or patch alone earns at most level 1.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Concept accuracy | No causal account, or account contradicts the executed sequence. | Gives a result, term, or repair without explaining its mechanism. | Explains the observed causal sequence and distinguishes client knowledge from persisted server state. | Also explains the correct identity lifecycle and the conditions under which the account holds. |
| Evidence linkage | No evidence, or invented evidence. | Relies on a toast or repeats AI advice. | Cites actual request/receipt and committed row IDs with the relevant code version. | Relates multiple observed statuses/identities and identifies what the evidence leaves unknown. |
| Boundary reasoning | No boundary or testable condition. | Repeats a happy path or slogan. | Distinguishes a retry of one intent from a permitted new intent and predicts relevant outcomes. | Proposes a discriminating conflict/concurrency test, predicts confirming and disconfirming observations, and states an assumption. |

Use actual scenario source and receipts to agree reference reasoning privately during rehearsal. Keep reference answer keys out of learner materials and this public repository. Do not infer understanding from successful API execution alone.

Two humans independently score both internal rehearsals and the first two external participants. Preserve initial values, then resolve disagreements with evidence IDs. If one person scores the remaining sessions, report that limitation. Do not claim inter-rater reliability from this small calibration.

Record a three-value profile and evidence references, with an assistance flag. Do not total values into a grade, percentage improvement, SOLO level, or mastery claim. There is no score-save UI or AI grading in this MVP. Exclude post-debrief repair from independent transfer scoring. Research eligibility is checked separately; research records contain only consented structured rubric values and other permitted structured measures.
