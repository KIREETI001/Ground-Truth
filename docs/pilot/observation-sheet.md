# Private observation sheet template

Copy one sheet per participant into the human owner's private records. Do not fill or commit this file. Use a pseudonym; keep contact details elsewhere. Observe behavior before interpreting it. Research records may contain only consented structured fields, never raw answers, quotes, transcripts, or notes.
