If interested, contact [human contact] with an available slot and any access needs. [Actual date, time, location/link, and incentive if applicable.]

The operator checks cleanup at pilot close and on the retention schedule. Expiry alone is not physical erasure. Downloaded copies need manual deletion; we cannot revoke them remotely or promise immediate removal from platform backups, native logs, or the provider.
