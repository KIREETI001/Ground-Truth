## The central story

Understand your code and architecture. Think in systems. Make the next change independently.

An AI-assisted take-home artifact can look stronger than its builder's independent understanding. That creates a teaching and assessment gap: the product alone does not show how a learner reasons, which concepts need attention or what they can do without in-app assistance. GroundTruth supplies inspectable practice evidence alongside the artifact. It supports responsible AI learning and informed human assessment.

The current MVP records in-app guidance and original attempts, links practice to concepts and supports explicit sharing. It does not detect outside AI, prove authorship, certify mastery or automatically assign grades. Teacher quiz authoring, assignment distribution and class-wide analytics are future work. Research motivates the problem; it does not validate this prototype's learning outcomes.
